# World at War (T4)

Only notes so far. There are no headers or OAT patches of our own for WaW. The T4 dumpers we added to OAT are in
[`shared/oat-patches/oat_ext/`](../../shared/oat-patches/oat_ext/).

| Folder | What is in it |
| --- | --- |
| `notes/zombies/` | Nacht der Untoten in detail (AI, round flow, sounds, weapons and points), Verrückt entities, and what the later maps add |
| `notes/mp/` | Team Deathmatch and Free-for-All rules |
| `notes/engine/` | player movement, hits and damage, viewmodel, effects and sound |

Every note is listed with its title in [NOTES.md](../../NOTES.md), which also explains the `[V]` `[M]` `[I]` `[U]`
marks.
